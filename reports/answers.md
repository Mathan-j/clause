# Answering evaluation

Generated by `make answer-eval`. Every figure here is produced by that command from the committed golden and adversarial sets; nothing in this file is estimated.

## Citation resolution

**The citation contract rejected 8.3% of generated drafts.** 0.9167 of drafts survived enforcement and were returned as answers; the rest cited something that did not resolve, or asserted a fact with no citation at all, and were discarded rather than returned. **This is the contract working, not failing.** A returned `Answer` carrying an unresolvable citation is impossible by construction -- `validate.enforce` raises before an `Answer` is built -- so this figure measures how often the model produced something the corpus would not support, and how often that was caught. It is a property of the generator, not of the enforcement path.

## Support signal

This is a term-overlap proxy, not entailment -- it catches a sentence citing a passage it shares no vocabulary with, and misses a fluent paraphrase that reverses the meaning.

Computed over **115** factual sentence(s) produced at the default threshold: mean **0.822**, **93** at or above 0.5, **2** at zero.

## Limitations

- The golden set (`data/golden/kyc-v1.jsonl`) is model-drafted, not human-verified -- every figure measured against it inherits that provenance, the same caveat Phase 2's `reports/eval.md` states.
- `effective_date` is absent for every document in this corpus (see `clause.answering.schema.Citation`'s docstring), so no citation can state when the rule it quotes actually took effect -- only when the circular carrying it was published.
- This is a term-overlap proxy, not entailment -- it catches a sentence citing a passage it shares no vocabulary with, and misses a fluent paraphrase that reverses the meaning. It is reported as a distribution and is not a faithfulness score, specifically so it cannot be quoted as one.
- The adversarial set (`data/adversarial/out-of-corpus-v1.jsonl`) holds a small number of questions per kind; its refusal rate should be read with the error bars that size implies, not as a precise percentage.
